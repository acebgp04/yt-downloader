How to download a YouTube video

  1. Open YouTube and find the video you want.
  2. Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar, or tap Share → Copy Link.
  3. Paste the link into the box above.
  4. Choose MP4 (video) or MP3 (audio only) and pick a resolution.
  5. Click Download — the file starts processing immediately with a live progress bar.
  6. Click the download link when it's ready.

Frequently asked questions

Is this YouTube downloader free?

Yes. Free accounts get unlimited downloads of videos under 30 minutes, plus a few longer videos per day. Subscribing removes the daily limit and unlocks playlists and chapter downloads.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in any mobile browser on iPhone or Android — no app or extension to install. Paste the link, choose a format, and download.

What video quality can I download?

Up to the best quality available for that video, typically 1080p, with lower resolutions also offered. You can also extract audio only as an MP3.

Do you keep my downloaded videos?

No. Files are deleted from our servers shortly after you download them — nothing is kept longer than necessary.

Can I download a whole YouTube playlist?

Yes, with a subscription. Free accounts can download single videos only.

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

Only download videos you have the right to download — your own content, content in the public domain, or content the uploader has given you permission to save. Respect copyright and YouTube's terms of service.
