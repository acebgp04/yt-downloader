How to convert a YouTube video to MP3

  1. Open YouTube and find the video you want.
  2. Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar, or tap Share → Copy Link.
  3. Paste the link into the box above — MP3 is selected by default.
  4. Click Download — the file starts converting immediately with a live progress bar.
  5. Click the download link when it's ready.

Frequently asked questions

Is this YouTube to MP3 converter free?

Yes. Free accounts get unlimited conversions of videos under 30 minutes, plus a few longer videos per day. Subscribing removes the daily limit and unlocks playlists.

What audio quality do I get?

MP3 files at 192kbps.

Can I convert a whole YouTube playlist to MP3?

Yes, with a subscription. Free accounts can convert single videos only.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in any mobile browser on iPhone or Android — no app or extension to install.

Do you keep my converted files?

No. Files are deleted from our servers shortly after you download them — nothing is kept longer than necessary.

Is it legal to convert YouTube videos to MP3?
